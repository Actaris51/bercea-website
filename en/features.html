Features

Bercea at a glance

An app built to answer the real questions parents have. No account required.

01 — Prediction

Smart sleep timer

Bercea starts a timer the moment your baby wakes up and alerts you before the end of the wake window. No more watching the clock every five minutes.

  • One-tap start
  • Alert before the fatigue point
  • 7-day nap history
02 — Personal algorithm

A model that learns your baby

The first predictions rely on age-based scientific averages (WHO, AASM). Then, nap after nap, the app gradually tunes the wake windows to your child's actual rhythm, up to 70% personal data after a month of tracking.

  • Blends scientific data with your baby's personal rhythm
  • Adapts to developmental transitions
  • Accounts for day/night rhythm from 12 weeks
  • Detects sleep debt and short naps
03 — Soothing sounds

10 sounds to fall asleep in minutes

From white noise to whale songs, each sound was picked for its documented calming effect. Background playback and hearing protection included.

  • White, pink, womb, rain, ocean…
  • Background playback
  • Built-in timer
  • Alert above 50 dB
04 — Parental well-being

A calmer parent for a calmer baby

Bercea also tracks your mood, energy and sleep. If the warning signs add up, the app points you to the right resources, without judgment.

  • Quick daily check-ins
  • Caring alerts
  • Validated external resources
05 — Built-in science

Studies, one tap away

Every recommendation links back to its scientific source. Bercea doesn't just tell you what to do — it explains why. Content is refreshed with the latest research (2024-2025).

  • Over 35 scientific studies referenced
  • Articles updated with recent research
  • Topic-based explainers
06 — Full history

Every nap and night, charted

Weekly chart, daily averages, deviation from the WHO/AASM norm for the exact age, and detail for each nap. See trends over 7 days, not just last night.

  • Interactive 7-day chart
  • WHO / AASM norm comparison
  • Per-nap detail (quality, duration)
  • Fix any entry with a single tap
07 — Bedtime routine

The only truly proven intervention

Among all sleep practices, only one has solid scientific evidence: the consistent bedtime routine. A 2024 clinical study of 306 infants showed a significant gain in nighttime sleep for babies with a routine compared to those without.

  • Customizable checklist: bath, massage, story…
  • 7-day consistency bar
  • Streak counter (consecutive days)
  • No judgment — the goal is consistency, not perfection

Free, forever

Because your baby's sleep shouldn't depend on your budget. No account, no data collected.
