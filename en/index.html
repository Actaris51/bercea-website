From day one, Bercea suggests time slots based on scientific averages. After a few naps, predictions adapt to your child's unique pattern.

  • Age-based wake windows
  • Learns in just a few days
  • Alerts before the fatigue point
Soothing sounds

A research-backed sound library. Background playback, built-in timer, and volume alert to protect your baby's fragile ears.

  • 10 sounds: white, pink, womb, rain, ocean…

Parental well-being

Because you matter too

An exhausted parent who ignores the signs eventually burns out. Bercea tracks your sleep, mood and energy, and points you to real resources when the warning signs build up.

  • Mood / energy / sleep tracking
  • Caring alerts
  • Validated external resources
Health record

Vaccines, growth and starting solids, in one place

Log each vaccine with its dose and batch number, follow growth, and keep track of starting solids. On appointment day, a PDF report to show the doctor, created on your phone.

  • Over 100 foods already named, by category
  • Allergens shown automatically
  • A counter for each food, search from the first letters
Nights

At night, one tap is enough

Night mode keeps the screen dark and the essentials within thumb's reach. When nights stay broken, Bercea suggests a sleep check-up: a few questions, then a plan that fits your child.

  • A night waking logged in one tap
  • A sleep check-up, answers kept on your phone
  • Medical warning signs always come first
Gentler together

The same rhythm, on both phones

You log a nap, the other parent sees it instantly. No more "how long did they sleep?" in the middle of the night.

  • Shared tracking between co-parents
  • An 8-digit invitation, valid for 15 minutes
  • Always in sync, on both sides
  • One less mental load
